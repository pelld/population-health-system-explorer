About

A connected operational system map

The map starts with routes into A&E, but it is not limited to A&E. It connects urgent contacts, primary care, ambulance activity, hospital admission, bed use, discharge, repeat use and the wider factors that shape them.

It is deliberately not a Sankey diagram. Route badges show counts and percentages from the public 2024–25 annual ECDS attendance-source table; line width does not represent volume.

Transparency

Sources, definitions and interpretation

Operational datasets use different units and definitions. Source of referral, arrival mode and previous service contact are related but not interchangeable. Comparisons must show data completeness and denominator definitions.
