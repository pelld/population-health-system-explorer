About

A determinants map, not a prediction

The central outcome is A&E attendance. The larger circles are direct determinants that may lead to attendance; the smaller circles are upstream factors that help create those determinants.

Management responses, timescales and ownership are shown in the details drawer. The map does not label attendance as inappropriate and does not prove that changing a determinant will reduce attendance.

Sources, evidence and interpretation

Official data sources support measurement and definitions. Some causal links are published evidence; others are explicit hypotheses that should be tested locally rather than treated as established fact.
