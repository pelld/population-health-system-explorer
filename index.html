About

A map for management questions, not a prediction

The map begins with a pressure—high or rising A&E attendance—and separates possible explanations. It is designed to help managers move from a headline measure to locally testable hypotheses and proportionate actions.

Timescale describes how quickly a lever might plausibly be influenced, not how large its effect will be. The map does not label attendance as inappropriate and does not prove that any intervention will reduce it.
