About

A map for thinking, not a causal model

The conceptual map brings together plausible relationships across population health, healthcare and wider determinants. Arrows mean “may influence”, unless a relationship is explicitly described as arithmetic.

The evidence view separates what is directly measured, what is represented by a proxy and what remains unknown. Future versions can connect verified public data for selected ICBs.

Transparency

Sources, evidence and interpretation

No arrow should imply more certainty than the evidence supports. Each driver is labelled as published evidence, an official definition, a plausible mechanism to test, or a known gap.
